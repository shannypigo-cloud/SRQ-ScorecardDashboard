Connect your scorecard

Connect the Google Sheet behind your scorecard through Apps Script. The dashboard can refresh automatically whenever new responses are submitted.

Recommended Apps Script setup:
1. Open your scorecard spreadsheet and choose Extensions → Apps Script.
2. Paste the provided Code.gs script and save it.
3. Select Deploy → New deployment → Web app.
4. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone.
5. Copy the deployment URL ending in /exec and paste it above.

Your spreadsheet does not need to be publicly shared. However, anyone with the endpoint URL can read its returned scorecard data.

Expected columns include Date, Time, VSA, DRIVERS, LARGE, SUV, SEDAN, SPECIAL, PICK UP, RTN, QTA DIRTY, QTA CLEAN, and RL.
